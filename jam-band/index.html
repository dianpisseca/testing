About this device

Record 30 to 60 seconds of your guitar with any recorder, or film yourself playing, then choose the file here. The band is added to your playing and you listen back.

Strum steadily for the first ten seconds or so. A clean sound works best. A .m4a, .mp3 or .wav file is fine.

On an iPhone: record in Voice Memos, tap Share, Save to Files, then choose it here. Or film yourself with the Camera app and pick the video.

With a fixed tempo the first strum is beat 1.
